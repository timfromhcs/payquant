PayQuant (PQN)

The World's First Post-Quantum, AI-Optimized Blockchain Architecture. Protected by NIST FIPS 204 ML-DSA-65 Cryptography and Synergeia Hybrid Consensus.

Architectural Foundations

🔐 NIST FIPS 204 ML-DSA-65 Cryptography

Every transaction and wallet address (`pqn1q...`) is secured by post-quantum lattice-based digital signatures. PayQuant guarantees protection against quantum decryption threats and Shor's algorithm.

⚡ Synergeia Hybrid Consensus

Combines Proof-of-Work (RinHash) block proposal with 27-node Proof-of-Stake consensus verification, delivering deterministic 15-second block finality with super-exponential safety margins.

🌐 Zero-Server P2P & Furthest Node Sync

Operating without central points of failure, PayQuant nodes discover global peers over multi-channel IRC signaling and synchronize with the network's furthest valid node to prevent chain splits.

⛏️ Direct Solo P2P Mining (RinHash)

Miners retrieve mining templates directly from P2P nodes without central pools. Memory-hard RinHash PoW rewards (50.0 PQN) are paid directly to the miner's wallet address.

💳 SPV Light Wallet & 24-Word BIP-39

The SPV Light Wallet functions independently without requiring a full node. Secured by a 24-word BIP-39 quantum backup seedphrase with integrated QR code payments and custom invoice requests.

💾 Persistent LevelDB / Chainstate Engine

High-performance block and transaction storage built on LevelDB and SQLite state indexes, ensuring full state persistence across process restarts and 1-click ZIP backup exports.
